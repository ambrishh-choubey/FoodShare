How it works

1. Donors list surplus

Post the food type, quantity and how long it stays safe. Your pin appears on nearby NGO maps instantly.

2. NGOs find & claim

A live Leaflet map and distance-sorted list show what is close. One click claims it - no double bookings.

3. Pick up & feed

The NGO collects the food and marks it picked up. Donors watch the status update in real time.
